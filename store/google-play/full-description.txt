AIM — Before I Am
Awareness. Intention. Motion.

Start with where you are, what you want to change, and who you want to become. AIM helps turn that context into a practical starting plan.

• Organize your next steps with daily, weekly, and monthly planning.
• Update your circumstances and adjust your plan when life changes.
• Capture thoughts by typing or dictating, review the words, and send when ready.
• Track goals, projects, and check-ins in one personal workspace.
• Include sleep, movement, nutrition, stress, and recovery in your planning.
• Set an opportunity focus and open relevant search links.
• Personalize the experience with optional visuals and soundscapes. Audio starts only when you choose.

An internet connection is required for sign-in, cloud saving, and AI features. AI responses may be incomplete or incorrect; verify important information. AIM provides general planning and wellness information, not medical care, diagnosis, treatment, legal advice, financial advice, or emergency assistance.

Some features may require Premium when subscriptions become available. Purchases are currently disabled. Any future trial, price, renewal, and cancellation terms will be shown before purchase. There is no automatic charge in this testing build.

Privacy and account deletion are accessible from the app. Deleting an AIM account does not delete Google Drive exports or cancel a separate Google Play subscription.
